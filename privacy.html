Privacy policy

Adeboyelash Inc. handles personal information under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). This page explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Details you send through our forms: name, email address, your message and the program or topic you choose.
  • Basic, privacy-respecting visit statistics, if enabled, with no advertising profiles.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to you, process an Academy application, or follow up on a service request. We do not sell your information.

Who can see it

Form messages are delivered to our team inbox. Our form delivery provider processes the message to send it to us. We share information with others only where you ask us to, or where the law requires it.

Names and stories

We do not publish the names of clients, staff or applicants. Testimonials with real names are never used without written consent.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing Admin@adeboyelash.com.

Third-party links

Links such as WhatsApp lead to services with their own privacy rules. DuroForCanada is a separate non-profit with its own practices.

Review

This policy is a starting version and should be reviewed by the company's legal adviser before it is relied on.
